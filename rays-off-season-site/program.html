Your Road Back to Spring Training

This is the full off-season plan — four phases, five training days a week, built to take you from foundational capacity all the way to max-velocity, on-field transfer. Strength, plyometrics, and conditioning are programmed together and progress week over week inside each phase.

How to use this

  1. Open a phase from the menu on the left. Phase 1 is fully programmed and ready to train — Phases 2–4 will unlock here as the staff finalizes them.
  2. Pick your day. Inside a phase, use the day tabs (Monday–Friday) to see that day’s full session, including plyometrics and conditioning.
  3. Follow sets × reps and tempo exactly as written, and progress week to week — the same exercises get harder from Week 1 to Week 4.
  4. Hit conditioning for the day as listed at the top of each session — it’s programmed alongside the lift, not separate from it.
